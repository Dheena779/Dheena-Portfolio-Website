About

Reliable systems, built with a creative eye.

I am a Site Reliability Engineer with three years of experience keeping high-traffic cloud platforms available, observable and fast.

My work is production monitoring, incident response and root cause analysis across distributed AWS environments. I define and track SLIs and SLOs, tune alert thresholds so teams hear about real problems first, and support releases so go-lives stay clean.

I also bring a security-minded view from SIEM-based monitoring and log pipeline work in a SOC, and I build full applications of my own with AI support. Away from the screen I shoot photography and video, write scripts and edit.
